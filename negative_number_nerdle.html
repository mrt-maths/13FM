Negative Number Nerdle

Make a correct 5-tile equation.

How to play: Choose tiles rather than typing. Numbers run from −9 to −1 and 1 to 9; zero is not available. The equation must have the form a operation b = c. A negative number uses only one square, so examples such as −2 × −4 = 8, −2 + 4 = 2, 5 − −1 = 6 and −8 ÷ 4 = −2 fit the five-square format.
